Real-World Analogy Hook

Imagine walking into a master chef's bakery where pure sunlight, fresh water, and air are whipped together to create sweet treats. Plant leaves are nature's solar bakeries! Inside every microscopic cell, tiny engines called chloroplasts capture sunlight to make food.

Core Concept Breakdown

Photosynthesis Equation: Carbon Dioxide + Water + Sunlight ➡️ Glucose (Sugar) + Oxygen

  • Chlorophyll: Green pigment that absorbs red and blue light waves while reflecting green light.
  • Stomata: Microscopic pores beneath leaves that inhale CO₂ and exhale clean oxygen (O₂).
  • Glucose: Energy molecule stored by plants to grow roots, flowers, and sweet fruits.
